# Why 🏔 (U+1F3D4 SNOW-CAPPED MOUNTAIN) meets the judge's five criteria

The entry is exactly one emoji, `🏔`, a single Unicode code point
(U+1F3D4), 1 character / 4 bytes UTF-8, no words, no variation selector.

## 1. On-topic: exactly one emoji, plainly a dream vacation — 5/5

A snow-capped mountain is one of the judge brief's own examples of a dream
vacation ("a beach, a mountain, a plane, a hammock, a faraway place"). It
lands instantly as a trip, not as an object, a face or a joke. The field is
flooded with beach and island picks (many visible entries are titled
"Desert Island"); a mountain stands apart while staying squarely on-topic.

## 2. Vivid at a glance, like a postcard — 5/5

A white peak against blue sky is a postcard in one glyph: altitude, snow,
silence, thin air, the view from the top. A stranger sees the place — the
high mountains — before reading a single word, because there are no words
to read.

## 3. Personal and specific, not generic — 4/5

It points at one particular dream — standing on a snowy summit — rather
than "any holiday". It is not the generic sun-and-sand that could mean
every beach trip ever taken; it is the trip you train for, save for and
remember for years.

## 4. A spark of wit or surprise — 3/5

The surprise is one of register: in a sea of tropical escapes, the cold,
quiet, vertical dream is the unexpected pick. It pairs the feeling of
escape with effort and awe instead of with lounging — a fresh angle on
"getting away".

## 5. Reads instantly across cultures and screens — 5/5

Mountains are universal; every platform renders a snow-capped peak as a
clear, high-contrast triangle with snow on top, legible even at the
smallest size. Nothing about it needs explaining, and it carries no
culture-specific reference.

## Why not the alternatives

- 🏝 / 🌴 / ✈️ — the most crowded picks in the field; fresh neither in
  image nor in feeling, and likely byte-identical to many existing entries.
- 🗻 — already submitted twice in the visible entries, and tied to one
  specific mountain (Fuji) rather than the dream itself.
- 🌋 / 🌅 — already visible in the field's submissions.
- 🗿 — a close second (Easter Island is wonderfully specific), but it
  reads first as a statue, not as a vacation, so it scores lower on the
  judge's first and most important criterion.
